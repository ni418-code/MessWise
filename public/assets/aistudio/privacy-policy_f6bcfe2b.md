# Privacy Policy starter

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

This starter policy must be reviewed and customized by your institution before public use.

## Data we collect
The service may collect account email, display name, meal type, feedback date, category ratings, optional comments, and limited technical logs required to secure and operate the service.

## How we use it
Feedback is used to summarize student satisfaction and help authorized mess staff identify recurring food-service issues. The service should collect only data necessary for this purpose.

## Access and sharing
Students should be able to access their own submissions. Authorized mess staff may access feedback needed for their responsibilities. Aggregate reports should not expose student identities unnecessarily. Data should not be sold.

## Retention and deletion
The institution must set a retention period and explain how account deletion requests are handled. Backups may retain deleted data until the documented backup expiry date.

## Security
Authentication, database row-level security, access controls, and server-side secrets should be configured before launch. No service can guarantee absolute security.

## Contact
Replace with the responsible college or mess administrator contact.
